04 Trade questions

Questions from
the trade, answered.

Samples, timelines, artwork specs and the finer points of a volume negotiation — here’s what buyers usually ask before they commit.

Still weighing it up?

Send us your quantity, style and deadline. We’ll reply with real numbers — not a catalogue form.

Ask on WhatsApp

Yes. We produce 1–2 samples per style so you can check fabric, hardware and branding in hand before placing a bulk order. Samples are charged at a retail-less-15% rate plus freight; that amount is adjusted against confirmed orders of 500+ units. Standard sample turnaround is 5–7 working days after we receive your logo file and approved mock-up.

For 500–2,000 units, plan for 10–15 working days from artwork sign-off and advance receipt. Orders above 2,000 units or styles using embroidery typically take 15–22 working days. We book your production slot once the proof is approved, and dispatch within 48 hours of QC sign-off. If you have a hard launch date, tell us upfront — we offer an expedited line when capacity allows.

Send vector artwork in AI, SVG or PDF with fonts outlined. For photo-realistic sublimation, a 300 DPI PNG or JPG at print size works. Embroidery logos should include a DST or EMB file; our team can digitise one for you at a one-time charge. We always issue a placement mock-up for approval before bulk printing — no surprises on the floor.

Our prices are ex-factory Ahmedabad. Freight is billed at actuals based on carton count, weight, destination and service level — surface, express or air. We work with pan-India partners and can coordinate export documentation through your CHA. If you have a preferred transporter, we’ll pack to their specifications and share dimensions before dispatch. GST is additional.

Once you cross 500 units, pricing opens up to direct negotiation. At 2,000+ units we can assign a dedicated line, lock a rate for 90 days, stage deliveries across your launch calendar and hold safety stock by arrangement. For repeat programmes and annual contracts, we build a shared specification sheet so every reorder matches the last - same fabric, same stitch, same shade.
